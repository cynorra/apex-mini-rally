Child Safety & Zero-Tolerance Policy

Apex Mini Rally (package: com.cynorra.apexminirally) · Developer: Cynorra · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Cynorra maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and is committed to providing a safe gaming environment.

1. Offline Single-Player Design

  • Apex Mini Rally is a single-player offline 3D arcade racing game.
  • The app contains no user accounts, no chat, no messaging, no online multiplayer, no social profiles, and no user-generated content (UGC).
  • Users cannot upload images, text, audio, or interact with other players inside the app.

2. Protection of Minors & Reporting

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you have any child safety concern or wish to report an issue regarding our application or any displayed advertisement, please contact us immediately at eren.gp54@gmail.com.
